Brijuni day tour with a swimming stop: what to expect

During the day, several Pula operators run a cruise around the Brijuni islands with a stop to swim at St. Jerolim island, inside Brijuni National Park. It's the daytime counterpart to the evening dolphin cruise: more about swimming and scenery than wildlife.

Quick pick

  • Swimming, food and drinks in one afternoon: Vacation Istria

Good to know

  • St. Jerolim island is part of Brijuni National Park, so there is usually a small entry fee paid on site, separate from the boat ticket.
  • This is a daytime trip, so dolphin sightings are possible but less likely than on an evening cruise.
  • Bring your own snorkelling gear if you have it; boats don't usually provide it.
  • Bad weather can move or cancel a trip. Operators usually offer another date or a refund, but terms differ, so check them in your confirmation.
