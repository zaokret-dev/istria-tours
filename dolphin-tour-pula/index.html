Dolphin tours in Pula: which boat is right for you?

A resident community of bottlenose dolphins lives in the northern Adriatic around Pula and the Brijuni islands, and several local operators take visitors out to see them. They differ in boat size, atmosphere and departure point. Here is how four of them compare, so you can choose and send one request.

Quick pick

  • Food, drinks and a relaxed evening on board: Vacation Istria
  • Small group, close-up, German or Italian on board: Boreas
  • Central departure, relaxed sunset cruise: Boat Europa
  • Family or bigger group, or a swim stop: Arena and Ana

Good to know

  • The dolphins are wild and protected in Croatia. Boats watch them from a distance, so sightings are likely but never guaranteed.
  • Most dolphin trips last about 3 hours. Sunset trips around the Brijuni islands are the classic choice.
  • Bad weather can move or cancel a trip. Operators usually offer another date or a refund, but terms differ, so check them in your confirmation.
  • Bring sunglasses, sunscreen, a camera and a light jacket for the ride back.
